Sport, In Motion

Mission

From grassroots tournaments to league broadcasts, our sports unit captures the speed, strain and drama that make a game worth watching — and turns it into films, highlights and campaigns with real weight.

High-frame-rate capture, embedded crews and a sharp edit room let us tell the story of an athlete, a team or a season while it is still happening.

Every sprint, every save, every second — held in the frame and handed back to the fans who live for it.
Coverage Planning

Great sport media starts before the whistle. We scout the venue, plan coverage around the moments that decide a match, and brief every camera operator on the story we are chasing.

Pace, power and precision, read at a glance: the stadium from every angle that matters.

The Athlete's Story

The athlete's story rarely fits inside the scoreboard. Long-form profiles and behind-the-scenes series give audiences the person behind the performance and sponsors a narrative worth backing.
